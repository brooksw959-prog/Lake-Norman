How Stableford works

Modified Stableford rewards good holes without letting blow-ups ruin your round. Each player gets their full course handicap in strokes, distributed by hole handicap index.

Score each hole relative to your net par:

0Double bogey or worse (pick up and move on)
1Net bogey
2Net par
3Net birdie
4Net eagle
5Net albatross

Highest point total wins. The green dots on the scorecard show where you get strokes.

Saturday groupings
Option A: Balanced
Groups are auto-balanced from Friday results. Each group gets a mix of top, middle, and bottom finishers so Saturday stays competitive across all three groups.
Option B: Tiered by score
Groups are stacked by Friday finish. Bottom 4 go off first (1:00p), middle 3 next (1:10p), top 3 go off last (1:20p). Best players earn the latest tee time.
